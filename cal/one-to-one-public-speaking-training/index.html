What You Will Practise During Your Public Speaking Coaching

1.

Speaking Foundations

Start with the basics: your stance, gestures and body language. In our sessions, I’ll help you practise these skills, speak with more confidence and feel at ease in front of an audience. My one-on-one public speaking coaching gives you time to work on your own speaking style at your own pace.

2.

Breathing And Voice

Learn to manage your breathing, steady your voice and speak at a comfortable pace. These are all part of the work we do in public speaking coaching.

3.

Commitment To Your Message

Commitment is the first part of my CPV Method. I'll help you communicate your message with purpose, so your audience understands what you're saying and why it matters. We'll work on this through practical training and speaking exercises.

4.

Pacing And Pauses

Many people rush because silence feels uncomfortable. I'll help you slow things down, use pauses and give your words room to land. This is something we'll practise in my one to one public speaking coaching sessions.

5.

Vocal Variety

Work on volume, emphasis, intonation and expression. These speaking skills help your audience follow your ideas and make your delivery more natural through practical public speaking one on one coaching.

6.

Real Presentation Practice

We'll use your own speaking material during sessions. I'll help you find the right pace, make your message clear and practise handling the moments that might feel difficult. My aim is to help you feel ready before the event.

A Simple Coaching Journey

How One-on-One Public Speaking Coaching Helps You Improve

Step 1. Discuss Your Speaking Challenges

We'll start with a conversation about your speaking experience and goals. I'll ask about situations where you feel uncomfortable, such as presentations, interviews or meetings. This helps me understand what you're struggling with and shape my public speaking coaching around the support you need.

Step 2. Build Your Speaking Foundations

We'll work on your stance, breathing and gestures through practical exercises. I'll show you how your body and voice affect your delivery, then give you simple things to practise between sessions. Your individual public speaking training will start with these basic speaking skills.

Step 3. Practise the CPV Method

I'll introduce you to my CPV Method, which focuses on Commitment, Pacing and Variety. We'll practise each part using your own speaking material. My one-on-one public speaking coaching includes direct feedback and practical tasks to help you develop a speaking style that feels natural.

Step 4. Apply Your Skills

Now it's time to put your practice into action. We'll work on real meetings, presentations and professional situations, then review what's improved and what needs more attention. My aim with public speaking coaching is to help you feel confident speaking independently, without relying on constant guidance.
