What We'll Prepare

Speech Preparation And Delivery With A Speaking Coach

1. Understanding Your Speech Purpose

We'll begin by discussing why you're giving the speech and what you want people to understand. We'll consider your subject, audience and main message. My speech coaching helps you decide what needs to be included and what can be left out.

2. Writing And Structuring Your Speech

Once your main ideas are clear, we'll organise them into a logical structure. We'll work on your introduction, main points and conclusion. My public speaking coaching sessions will help you connect each part and create a speech that's easy to follow.

3. Creating A Strong Opening And Ending

Your opening gives people their first impression of your speech. Your ending helps them remember your message. We'll work on both parts and make sure they suit your subject. My speech coach guidance helps you prepare an opening and ending that feel natural.

4. Developing Your Speech Delivery

Writing a good speech is only part of the preparation. We'll work on pace, pauses, volume and vocal variety. My speech delivery coaching includes practical speech delivery techniques that help you develop good delivery in a speech.

5. Rehearsing Your Speech

We'll rehearse your speech and review how it sounds when spoken aloud. I'll help you identify difficult sections, improve transitions and make changes where needed. My presentation skills coaching focuses on practical feedback to help you prepare for the occasion.

6. Preparing For Your Audience

Every speech needs to suit its audience. I'll help you decide what to say, how much detail to include and which language feels right. My presentation consultant experience helps me guide you through these decisions.

Your Speech Preparation Process

Four Steps To Prepare Your Speech From Start To Finish

Step 1. Understanding Your Speech Requirements

We'll discuss your subject, audience, purpose and speaking date. You can bring your initial ideas, notes or an existing draft. I'll help you understand what your speech needs to communicate. This gives our public speaking coaching sessions a clear starting point.

Step 2. Planning And Structuring Your Speech

We'll organise your ideas and decide how your speech should flow. I'll help you develop your introduction, main points and conclusion. If you're preparing a business presentation, we can also consider how your speech fits into your wider presentation training requirements.

Step 3. Rehearsing Your Speech Delivery

Once your speech is ready, we'll rehearse it aloud. I'll listen to your delivery and give feedback on pace, pauses, voice and clarity. My speech delivery coaching helps you work through difficult sections and become more comfortable with your material.

Step 4. Reviewing And Improving Your Speech

We'll review your performance and make any final changes. You'll have time to repeat your speech and work on areas that need attention. My speech coaching helps you feel better prepared before speaking to your actual audience.
