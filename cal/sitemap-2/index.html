02 / Service architecture

Service pages: clear roles

Keep personal coaching and company training separate. Each service page should answer a different need.

INDIVIDUAL COACHING
One-to-One Public Speaking Coaching
Overall speaking confidence, delivery and audience connection.
Presentation Coaching
Business presentations, pitches, meetings and professional talks.
Speech Preparation
Preparing and rehearsing one specific speech, keynote or event.
CORPORATE TRAINING
Corporate Presentation Training
Presentation skills training for employees and teams.
Communication Workshops
Group workshops for workplace communication, if offered as a distinct format.
Leadership Communication Training
Communication coaching for managers and senior leaders, if offered.
Publishing check: Keep a page only if the service is genuinely distinct and has enough useful content. Combine offers that are almost the same.

05 / SEO rules & navigation

How this structure reduces overlap

Give every page a clear job. Similar pages should not compete for the same search.

1
One page, one main intent

Avoid several pages targeting the same service phrase.

2
Separate audience types

Personal coaching pages target individuals; corporate pages target organisations.

3
Separate service and method

Service pages explain the offer; CPV explains Cal’s approach.

4
Blogs answer questions

Articles link to the matching service page rather than copying its sales purpose.

5
Only list real services

Keep workshops and leadership training separate only if they are distinct offers.

6
Use helpful internal links

Link from relevant articles, proof pages and the homepage to service pages.
