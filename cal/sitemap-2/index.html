05 / Website structure

A focused structure for the website

The proposed sitemap keeps the website to nine main pages, with clear routes to each service and supporting content grouped where visitors expect to find it.

One clear navigation structure

Home, a Services dropdown with three direct service links, About Cal, Testimonials & Case Studies, Blog, Free Resources & Webinars, and Contact.

9 main pagesA focused website structure with no separate Services or FAQ page.
3 direct service pagesEach service has its own root-level URL and place in the Services dropdown.
Supporting informationFAQs sit on the Homepage and service pages. Privacy and Terms sit in the footer.
