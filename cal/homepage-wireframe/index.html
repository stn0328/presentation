05 / THE CPV METHOD

Less theory. More doing.

There are plenty of books, courses and online videos about public speaking. But knowing what makes a good speaker and actually becoming one are two different things.

My approach is simple: we practise. I use techniques from my theatre background to help people discover what works for them. You'll try different approaches, get feedback and practise again.

At the heart of my coaching is my own CPV Method. Three simple principles help you make more of your words.

C

Commitment

Say what you mean and mean what you say. Learn to speak with purpose, conviction and a clear message.

P

Pacing

Give your words room to breathe. Learn when to slow down, when to pause and how to let important points land.

V

Variety

Keep people listening. Use changes in tone, pace and vocal expression to make your message more interesting and engaging.

Together, these principles help you move from simply saying words to communicating something people remember.
