01 / MY STORY & APPROACH

From theatre direction to public speaking coaching.

I spent my early career directing theatre productions and coaching actors. During that time, I saw how practice, feedback and rehearsal could help people become more confident in the way they communicate.

I also noticed that professionals faced many of the same challenges. They rushed through their words, struggled to speak up or found it difficult to keep an audience interested.

In October 2024, I founded The Communication Catalyst to bring a director's approach to workplace communication.

My sessions use practical exercises, questions and honest feedback. We'll work with your own presentations and speaking challenges, rather than giving you a script to follow.

The aim isn't to make you sound like someone else. It's to help you become a stronger version of the speaker you already are.

02 / THE CPV METHOD

Three principles. A more effective way to speak.

At the centre of my coaching is the CPV Method: Commitment, Pacing and Variety. These three principles help you make more of your words and connect with the people listening.

Commitment

Say what you mean, and mean what you say.

Speak with purpose and conviction. Make your message clear so people understand what you're saying and why it matters.

Pacing

Give your words room to breathe.

Learn when to slow down, when to pause and how to give important points time to land.

Variety

Keep people listening.

Use changes in tone, pace and vocal expression to make your delivery more natural and engaging.

Together, these principles help you communicate clearly without losing your own style.
