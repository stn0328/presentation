Training Areas

The Main Skills Covered In My Corporate Communication Training Course

1. Understanding Your Audience

Who are you speaking to, and what do they need to understand? We'll work on these questions during professional communication training, helping you shape a message that's clear and easy to follow.

2. Voice And Delivery

The way you say something affects how people receive it. We'll work on breathing, pronunciation, pauses and vocal variety. Participants get time to practise these skills and understand how they can use them in everyday communications skills training.

3. Clear Presentations

A good presentation gives people a clear message to take away. I'll help you organise your ideas, improve your delivery and rehearse in your own words. That's what I focus on in my corporate communications training courses.

4. Communicating As A Leader

Managers often need to explain difficult decisions, guide teams and speak with confidence. My leadership communications training gives people time to practise these situations, consider their audience and find a way of speaking that feels clear and authentic.

5. Making Your Point

People need to explain ideas, respond to questions and influence decisions. Through influencing skills training, we'll practise how to make a point, support it and communicate with purpose without sounding forced or rehearsed.

6. Speaking Under Pressure

Important conversations don't always happen when you feel ready. We'll rehearse situations that participants may find challenging, review what happens and try again. These practical exercises can also support wider executive communication training.

How My Sessions Work

Four Steps To Better Communication Through Professional Communication Training

Step 1. Understand Your Workplace

I'll discuss your business, the people attending and the speaking challenges they face. We can look at the situations where better communication would help, including meetings, presentations and professional conversations. This discussion gives me a starting point for your corporate communications training.

Step 2. Plan Relevant Activities

I'll prepare a training plan around your goals and participants. My corporate communication training and workshops include practical activities based on real workplace situations, giving everyone a clear purpose for each exercise.

Step 3. Practise And Improve Delivery

Participants will practise through speaking exercises, rehearsal and workplace examples. I'll provide feedback on their voice, pace, clarity and delivery. They can repeat activities and try different approaches, helping them understand their speaking habits and work on areas that need attention.

Step 4. Review Your Learning

We'll review the skills participants have practised and discuss how to use them in their daily responsibilities. I'll identify areas where further practice may be useful, helping participants continue their development after the corporate communications training courses finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide corporate communication training for teams and individuals?

Yes. I provide corporate communication training through practical team workshops and one-to-one coaching for individuals. Team sessions focus on shared workplace challenges, while individual coaching gives you time to work on your own speaking goals.

Can the training course be customized to our business needs?

Yes. I shape each corporate communication training course around your team's needs. We can work on presentations, pitches, client conversations or speaking up in meetings, using real examples from your workplace.

Do you offer online and in-person corporate communication training?

Yes. I deliver team workshops in person by default, with online sessions available on request. Individual coaching is available at my Glasgow studio, at an agreed location, or online through Zoom or Teams. Each communication course is based on practical exercises and rehearsal.

Do you provide corporate communication training across the UK?

I deliver team workshops across Scotland, with online sessions available on request. If your organisation is based elsewhere in the UK, get in touch to discuss your requirements and arrange a suitable online session. My experience in public speaking coach work shapes the practical exercises I use in training.

Do you provide training for managers and business leaders?

Yes. I work with managers and business leaders on presentations, important conversations and speaking with confidence. My executive communication training uses practical exercises, rehearsal and honest feedback. I also include communication skill courses and communications skills training activities where they fit your team's needs.

How soon can we arrange a corporate communication training session?

The first step is to get in touch and discuss your team's needs. I offer two free 60-minute team tasters each month, with a decision-maker in the room. We can then discuss suitable dates for your corporate communication training.
