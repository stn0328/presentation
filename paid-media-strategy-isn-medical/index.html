    Purpose of this document

    This document sets out how we propose to use Google Ads and Meta Ads to grow online sales of Accu-Chek through ISN’s shop. It covers:

    • who we will target
    • the job each channel does
    • how we will structure campaigns
    • what needs to be in place before we scale spend
    • how budget will be phased
    • how we will measure success

    Ad copy, creative concepts and asset specifications will follow in a separate ad brief once this strategy is approved.

    Objectiveonline sales of Accu-Chek through ISN’s shop
    Audiencepatients and caregivers (B2C only; pharmacies and clinics are routed to a separate wholesale channel)
    ChannelsGoogle Search (already running) and Meta (new)
    1

    The strategy on one page

    Positioning
    “Genuine Accu-Chek, straight from the distributor.” Every competitor sells Accu-Chek as a commodity. Only ISN can say its stock comes direct from Roche’s distributor.
    Google’s job
    Capture people already searching. Accu-Chek brand searches total about 3,000 a month in Nigeria, and generic “glucometer” searches about 4,900. Volume is limited, so Google wins on efficiency, not scale.
    Meta’s job
    Create demand, retarget site visitors and drive repeat strip orders. This is where growth will come from. Competitors on Meta are small resellers and herbal-cure sellers, so a credible brand can stand out quickly.
    Main offer
    A starter kit (meter, strips and lancets) for new users, and a strip refill bundle for existing users.
    Profit driver
    Strips. A box of 50 lasts about 7 weeks at one test a day, and under 2 weeks at four a day. The meter brings the customer in; repeat strip orders make the money.
    Main metric
    Return on ad spend (ROAS) from tracked Paystack purchases. Supporting metrics: cost per purchase, conversion rate, average order value and repeat rate.

    What needs to be in place before we scale spend

    These are blockers. Ads sent to a page without prices, or without purchase tracking, will waste budget.

    1. Purchase tracking.
      • After a Paystack payment, the buyer must return to a thank-you page on isnmedical.com, and that page must record a purchase with the order value.
      • We will send that purchase event to GA4, Google Ads (with enhanced conversions) and Meta (Pixel plus Conversions API).
      • Without this, neither platform can learn who buys.
    2. The /accu-chek/ landing page, live with: Naira prices; delivery fee and delivery time; NAFDAC numbers; the warranty; WhatsApp support.
    3. Product catalogue on Meta. The six Accu-Chek products with prices and images. This powers carousel and retargeting ads.
    4. Google Shopping check. We will confirm in Google Merchant Center whether Shopping ads are available for Nigeria. If they are, we will add a product feed. Shopping ads show the price and photo before the click, which suits these buyers.
    5. Price decision. We recommend setting ISN’s retail price at or near the recommended retail price, so ISN doesn’t undercut its 36 pharmacy partners. Competitors sell Instant strips at ₦15,900–₦21,000, so ISN needs to know exactly where it sits.

    Google Ads planKeywords, bidding, locations and weekly checks

    Keyword settings

    • Exact and phrase match for the brand campaigns.
    • Generic searches get more room, with the search terms report reviewed weekly.
    • Negative keywords (searches we block): pregnancy, ovulation, urine, malaria, HIV, diagram, pictures, image, job, vacancy, PDF, India, UK, USA, Amazon.
    • “Wholesale” is a negative here. Those searches go to a wholesale campaign if ISN wants one.

    Bidding

    1. For the first 2–4 weeks, use Maximise conversions, optimising for purchases. If there are fewer than about 15 purchases a month, we optimise for “begin checkout” until volume builds.
    2. Move to Target ROAS (a target return on spend) once a campaign has 30 or more purchases in 30 days.

    Locations and timing

    • Target all of Nigeria.
    • Raise bids in Lagos, FCT and Rivers if those areas convert better.
    • Review the hour-of-day report after 4 weeks before cutting any hours.

    Weekly checks

    • Review the search terms report and add negatives.
    • Check Auction Insights to see which competitors (HealthPlus, Asset Pharmacy, GoMed, Jumia) bid on ISN’s terms.
    • Confirm landing page URLs work, given the July URL change.

    Measurement and reporting

    Tagging

    Every ad link carries UTM tags (channel, campaign and ad), so GA4 reports match each platform’s own numbers.

    Weekly checks

    Spend, purchases, cost per purchase, ROAS, conversion rate, how often each audience sees an ad, and how quickly each creative is wearing out.

    Monthly report

    Paid media results added to your existing monthly report from Pedicel, including new versus returning buyers and the strip repeat rate.

    Testing

    One change per test, run for at least 2 weeks or until 50 or more conversions per version. Test order:

    1. creative concept
    2. offer (starter kit vs single meter)
    3. WhatsApp vs website
    4. landing page variations
    10

    Rules and risks

    RiskHow we manage it
    Meta health ad rulesAds never say or imply the viewer has a condition (“Do you have diabetes?”, “Your sugar is high”). They talk about the product and the action. No claims of cures or outcomes.
    Health claims generallyA glucometer monitors blood sugar; it doesn’t treat anything. The “doesn’t replace your doctor” note stays on the landing page.
    Stock and expiryOnly products in stock, with long expiry dates, are advertised. Ads pause automatically if stock runs out.
