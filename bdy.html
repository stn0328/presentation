Month 1 – Foundation & Technical SEO Fixes

1. Website Audit Report

  • Run full technical SEO audit
  • Identify crawl errors, broken links, redirect chains
  • Set up & configure Yoast SEO
  • Improve load speed (enable caching, compress scripts/images)
  • Mobile-friendliness check
  • Check for schema markup issues

2. Keyword Research Report

  • Detailed business and competitor analysis
  • Find primary, long-tail, and geo-based keywords
  • Map keywords to pages (local & national focus)
  • Create a keyword targeting sheet
03

Month 1 – Page Optimization & Tracking

3. Page Optimization (3 pages)

  • Add keywords to title tags, H1s, meta descriptions, image alt text, and body text
  • Compress images and add Alt text with relevant keywords
  • Write SEO-friendly meta descriptions under 155 characters
  • Ensure 300+ words per page with subheadings and proper structure
  • Use Yoast readability suggestions for tone and clarity:
    Active voice
    Proper heading structure
    Simple words
  • Add internal links to other pages
  • Link to trustworthy external sources

4. Tracking Setup

  • Connect MS Clarity (email: ksikandarali76@gmail.com)
  • Install event tracking for phone clicks, form submissions, booking actions
  • Create a basic performance Report
04

Month 2 – Content Growth & Local SEO

1. Google My Business Optimization

  • Add keyword-rich business description
  • Add services & products with clear categories
  • Upload geotagged images of staff, rooms, services
  • Ensure all business info (NAP) is consistent
  • Post 2 GMB posts per month (specials, FAQs, updates)
  • Add FAQs directly in the GMB profile

2. Local SEO: Location-Based Pages (1st page)

  • Create 1 suburb-targeted service page (e.g. Body Contouring in Southport)
  • Use geo keywords in title, H1, meta tags, and throughout content
  • Write 500+ words with clear service details
  • Add embedded Google Map and structured data (LocalBusiness schema)
  • Add reviews/testimonials from clients in that area

3. Basic Content Marketing

  • Plan 3 blog ideas based on keyword gaps (draft titles/topics)
  • Complete 3 Blogs Content
05

Month 3 – Content Growth & More Local SEO

1. Google My Business (Ongoing)

  • Post 2 more GMB updates (before/after photos, promos)
  • Monitor insights (calls, direction requests, views)
  • Respond to GMB reviews

2. Local SEO: Second Location Page

  • Create 1 more location-specific service page (e.g. Cryotherapy in Broadbeach)
  • Target suburb-focused keywords
  • Include embedded map, geo images, and testimonials
  • Add schema markup and optimize metadata

3. Publish 3 Blogs

  • Finalize & publish blog posts (focus on a long-tail transactional keyword)
  • Add images, alt text, meta title & meta description
  • Interlink with service/location pages

4. Link Building

  • List business on 5–10 local directories (True Local, StartLocal, Yelp AU)
  • Request 1–2 backlinks from niche/local blogs
