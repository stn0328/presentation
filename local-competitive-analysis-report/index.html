Metrics guide

How to read the numbers in this report

Main keyword and search volume (SV)
The search phrase each competitor is built to win, and how many people search it each month.
Position on top keyword
Where the site appears in Google for that keyword. 1 is the top spot.
Authority Score (AS)
A 0–100 score for the overall strength of a site’s SEO. Higher means harder to outrank.
Backlinks
Links from other websites pointing to the site. Google treats them as votes of trust.
Search traffic
Estimated visits arriving from Google search each month.
Blogs and total pages
The number of blog articles and of all pages on the site. More pages mean more ways to be found.
Google Business Profile (GBP)
The free Google Maps listing. We counted each competitor’s profiles, their GBP keywords and linked every profile.
GBP posts
Updates published on the profile: the total so far and how often they post each month.
Reviews and ratings
The number of Google reviews and the average star rating out of 5.
Founded year
When the business started. A second year in brackets is shown as listed in the sheet.
Services
What each competitor offers, from airport transfers to courier and wedding cars.
Areas and focused niche
Where they say they operate, and the main market they concentrate on.

Takeaways

Five opportunities this data points to

What the data showsWhat to do

Win the “near me” searches

7 of 18 competitors target near-me terms, yet only 5 of 16 post to their Google profile 2–3 times a month or more.

Keep your Google Business Profile complete, post regularly and ask for reviews.

Build review proof

The median rating is 4.5★ and four competitors sit below 4★. Goldline Cars sets the benchmark: 5,790 reviews at 4.6★.

Collect reviews steadily and reply to every one.

Publish more useful pages

Heavyweights average 869 pages against 113 for small players, and 4 of the 5 biggest traffic earners run blogs of 58 to 640 articles.

Add service, area and airport pages, plus a regular blog.

Claim the thin niches

Few competitors cover school runs (3), meet and greet (3), accessible or child-seat rides (4), London City (3) or Southend airport (2).

Build dedicated pages for the niches that fit your fleet.

Start with winnable keywords

9 of the 13 #1 rankings are on keywords under 3K searches a month, and 3 of the 4 newest sites already rank #1.

Win specific keywords first, then move up to bigger ones.
