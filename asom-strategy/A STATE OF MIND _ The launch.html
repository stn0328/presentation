The idea05

Five cities, three conversations

Fewer, larger groups help the platforms learn faster on a launch budget. We still report every city on its own.

Dubai, London

In English, for the Gulf and Europe

Miami, Los Angeles

In English, for the United States

Paris

In French, with its own ads

With a launch budget, splitting into five separate campaigns would spread money too thin. Grouping cities lets the algorithm learn faster, and we can still see every city in reporting. California starts with Los Angeles and Orange County; we can add the Bay Area later.
