Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Sirius AI Lab?

Sirius AI Lab is a global business membership for owners who want to grow their businesses with stronger commercial thinking, practical AI tools, and a clear sense of purpose.

2. Why was Sirius AI Lab created?

Lynn and Garry wanted to create a space where business strategy and purpose could work together. They believed business owners needed practical commercial knowledge without losing sight of what matters to them.

3. How is Sirius AI Lab different from other business communities?

Sirius AI Lab brings commercial business experience and conscious thinking into the same conversation. It puts real business decisions, financial understanding, and collaboration at the heart of its approach.

4. How does AI fit into Sirius AI Lab?

AI is part of the technology behind Sirius AI Lab. The team creates custom AI tools designed by experienced business operators to help solve everyday business challenges.

5. Do I need to understand finance to become a member?

No. You don't need to be a finance expert. Understanding things like cash flow, pricing, profit margins, and balance sheets can help you make better business decisions. These are practical areas of business knowledge, not just topics for accountants.
