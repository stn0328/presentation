Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Sirius AI Lab Membership?

Sirius AI Lab Membership gives founders, business owners and investors access to AI tools, business resources, education and networking opportunities. Your benefits depend on the membership plan you choose.

2. What is included in Sirius AI Lab Business Membership?

It includes AI Concierge, business agents, templates, learning content and private community access. Higher plans add Sirius Exchange, business education, networking events and investment-related participation.

3. Which membership plan should I choose?

Foundation is suited to early-stage founders. Growth Syndicate is for established business owners who want more business resources. Inner Circle is designed for established founders, scale-up leaders and active angel investors.

4. Can I apply to the Sirius AI Lab Investment Fund?

Yes. Foundation members are eligible to apply to the AED 1,000,000 fund. Applications go through selection and due diligence, so funding is not guaranteed.

5. What are the Sirius AI Lab Inner Circle benefits?

Inner Circle includes Sirius Exchange PRO, an Angel Investor Panel seat, voting and deal-selection input, an annual retreat, a private founder dinner, Sirius Alliance and high-net-worth peer-group meetings.

6. What is the Sirius AI Lab Growth Syndicate?

Growth Syndicate is a membership for established founders and business owners. It includes Sirius Exchange, Sirius Business School, live masterclasses, networking events, media opportunities and cross-member trading.

7. Does every plan include Sirius Exchange?

No. Growth Syndicate includes Sirius Exchange, while Inner Circle includes Sirius Exchange PRO. Foundation members do not receive access to either version.

8. Can I change or cancel my membership?

You can contact the team to discuss changing or cancelling your membership. Changing plans means new pricing and terms apply. Cancellation also means losing your locked joining rate.

9. How do I access the membership benefits?

Once your membership is confirmed, the team will arrange access to the platforms and resources included in your plan. You'll then be able to start using your available member benefits.

10. How can I check Sirius AI Lab Pricing?

Foundation costs AED 750 per month, Growth Syndicate costs AED 2,500 per month and Inner Circle costs AED 150,000 per year. Contact our team if you have questions about the plans.
