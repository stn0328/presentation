Your Member Benefits

Six Ways Sirius AI Lab Supports Your Business

Your membership gives you access to six areas of support. The benefits available depend on the membership level you choose.

AI Concierge and Business Agents

Use your AI concierge and business agents for member matching and client discovery. Share what your business offers and the type of connections you're looking for. These tools help identify relevant people and potential opportunities for your business.

Business Templates Library

Access ready-to-use business templates for your company. Use them to organise tasks, prepare business documents and plan your work. You can choose the templates you need and adapt them to suit your business.

Founder Masterclasses

Learn from experienced founders through pre-recorded masterclasses and digital content. Growth Syndicate members get access to Sirius Business School, with further learning resources to support their business knowledge and development.

Sirius Exchange

Growth Syndicate members receive access to Sirius Exchange, including marketing, sales and social media linking tools. Inner Circle members get Sirius Exchange PRO, which includes an enterprise-grade marketing and listing suite.

Private Founder Community

Become part of a private community of business owners. Connect with other members, share ideas and request introductions. Your membership may include access to in-person events, giving you more opportunities to meet fellow founders.

Inner Circle Access

Inner Circle members receive an Angel Investor Panel seat, an annual retreat and a private founder dinner. You can take part in deal-selection discussions, with optional co-investment rights subject to regulatory compliance. Investment opportunities are not guaranteed.

Faqs

What AI labs solutions can I access through Sirius AI Lab?

Our AI labs solutions include business agents, an AI concierge and practical business resources. These tools help your AI powered company find relevant connections, identify potential clients and use AI in everyday business tasks.

Can I access Sirius AI Lab online?

Yes. Sirius AI Lab online gives members access to their available tools and resources through their membership. You can use our AI laboratory resources, business templates and learning content without needing to visit a physical location.

Is Sirius AI Lab available in Dubai and across the UAE?

Sirius AI Lab gives business owners in Dubai and across the UAE access to practical AI tools, business resources and founder connections. Our AI lab Dubai and AI lab UAE membership options make these resources available to local entrepreneurs looking to use AI in their businesses.

What makes Sirius AI Lab an artificial intelligence laboratory for businesses?

Sirius AI Lab gives businesses access to an AI concierge, business agents and practical resources. As an artificial intelligence laboratory, we focus on helping founders use AI for client discovery, member matching and everyday business tasks.

Does Sirius AI Lab develop AI products and software?

Sirius AI Lab gives members access to practical AI tools, including business agents and an AI concierge. If you run an AI product company or want to use AI-powered products in your business, our membership provides tools and resources to support your work.

What is the difference between AI build labs and AI systems labs?

AI build labs focus on creating and testing AI solutions, while AI systems labs focus on developing AI systems that work together to perform specific tasks. Both can involve AI-powered software, depending on the tools and solutions being developed.
