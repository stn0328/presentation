Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Technical SEO Audit & Rank Tracking Setup

Technical SEO Audit

  • Run a thorough technical SEO audit to uncover issues affecting crawling, indexation, and site structure.
  • Look for inconsistent URLs, duplicate pages, broken links, redirect chains, and orphan pages.
  • Review mobile usability, HTTPS security, and other technical factors that impact search visibility.
  • Order the issues found by how much they affect crawling, indexing, performance, and user experience.

SEO Tools & Rank Tracking Setup

  • Add the project to a rank tracking tool.
  • Configure the website along with its target location.
  • Input the target keywords and capture their initial rankings.
  • Enable competitor tracking where it applies.
Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Phase 1 · Technical SEO

WordPress SEO Plugin, XML Sitemap & Robots.txt

WordPress SEO Plugin Optimization

  • Audit the Rank Math setup and SEO settings.
  • Improve the global metadata settings.
  • Set up indexing rules for pages and posts.
  • Confirm SEO settings are correct across key website pages.

XML Sitemap & Robots.txt Optimization

  • Check and refine the XML sitemap and robots.txt directives.
  • Confirm that key indexable pages appear in the sitemap and are accessible to search engines.
  • Leave out unsuitable or non-indexable URLs from the sitemap where required.
  • Submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and check that it has been processed.
Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Performance, Core Web Vitals & Schema Markup

Website Performance & Core Web Vitals

  • Review website speed and Core Web Vitals scores.
  • Pinpoint performance problems linked to images, scripts, CSS, and page loading.
  • Propose and carry out improvements where feasible.
  • Re-test performance once optimization is complete.

Structured Data & Schema Markup

  • Audit existing structured data and identify missing or incorrect schema types.
  • Implement relevant schema markup based on the website's content and business.
  • Validate structured data and resolve implementation errors.
  • Verify that schema markup accurately represents the information on each page.
Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Technical Fixes & Quality Assurance

  1. 1

    Apply

    Apply the priority technical fixes found during the audit.

  2. 2

    Confirm

    Confirm crawling, indexation, redirects, canonical tags, and internal links work correctly after the changes.

  3. 3

    Re-test

Phase 2 · On-Page SEO & Content Production

Service Pages & Homepage

Service Pages: Research, Content Writing & Publishing

13service pages
  • Research service-specific keywords, search intent, coverage details, and competing broker pages for all 13 service pages.
  • Write unique service page content covering what each policy covers, who it is for, the claims process, and FAQs, with optimized headings and relevant internal links.
  • Publish optimized content using the appropriate Elementor template.
  • Review service information, content quality, page formatting, and indexability.

Homepage: Research, Content Writing & Optimization

  • Research primary keywords, search intent, and competing Nigerian insurance broker homepages.
  • Rewrite the homepage title tag, meta description, and main heading to clearly target insurance brokerage in Nigeria.
  • Optimize homepage content, headings, and internal links to all service pages.
  • Review the published homepage for content quality, layout, and indexability.

Phase 3 · Off-Page SEO

Phase 3 · Off-Page SEO

Backlink Audit, Link Building & Digital PR

Backlink Audit & Competitor Analysis

  • Check current backlinks and judge their quality and relevance.
  • Investigate suspicious backlink patterns and potentially damaging links.
  • Assess competitor backlink profiles and referring domains.
  • Spot backlink gaps and rank the most realistic link-building opportunities.

Link Building & Digital PR

  • Discover relevant link-building opportunities across the industry.
  • Contact relevant websites, publishers, and industry resources through outreach.
  • Earn relevant editorial links and develop genuine industry relationships.
  • Keep track of acquired backlinks and referring domain growth.
Phase 3 · Off-Page SEO

Phase 3 · Off-Page SEO

Web 2.0 Link Building & Local Citations

Web 2.0 Link Building

  • Select suitable third-party publishing platforms for branded content.
  • Build and refine branded profiles with consistent business information.
  • Share valuable, original content covering the business and its areas of expertise.
  • Place contextual website links naturally where relevant and permitted by each platform's guidelines.

Local Citations & Business Listings

  • Identify relevant Nigerian business directories, insurance industry associations, and broker listing platforms.
  • Create or optimize business listings, including pages such as insurance broker directories and partner networks.
  • Maintain a consistent business name, Lagos office address, phone number, and website link across all listings.
  • Review existing citations and identify missing listing opportunities in the Nigerian market.
Phase 3 · Off-Page SEO

Phase 3 · Off-Page SEO

Social Profiles, Google Business Profile & Reviews

Social Profile Optimization

  • Identify relevant social media platforms for the business, prioritizing professional networks alongside the existing profiles.
  • Create or optimize business profiles with consistent branding, bio, logo, and messaging that reflect the company's expertise and professional identity.
  • Add website links to key service pages and the Insights section, along with relevant business details.
  • Ensure profiles accurately represent the company and its insurance services.

Google Business Profile & Reviews

  • Review and optimize the Google Business Profile, where applicable.
  • Verify business information, categories, services, and descriptions.
  • Publish regular Google Business Profile posts featuring company updates, products, services, and relevant announcements.
  • Add suitable business photos and supporting information.
  • Develop a process for requesting and responding to genuine customer reviews.
  • Monitor profile accuracy, engagement, and local search performance.
