Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Audit & Baseline Setup

Workstreams 1.1 – 1.2
1.1

Technical SEO Audit

  • Carry out a complete technical SEO audit to identify issues affecting crawling, indexation, and site structure.
  • Review the site for inconsistent URLs, duplicate pages, broken links, redirect chains, and orphan pages.
  • Evaluate mobile usability, HTTPS security, and other technical factors that affect search visibility.
  • Prioritize the issues found based on their impact on crawling, indexing, performance, and user experience.
1.2

SEO Tools & Rank Tracking Setup

  • Set up a new project in a rank tracking tool.
  • Configure the website and its target location.
  • Enter the target keywords and record their baseline rankings.
  • Add competitor tracking where applicable.
Pedicel Marketing · 4-Phase SEO Strategy Report
Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Metadata & Index Control

Workstreams 1.3 – 1.4
1.3

Metadata Optimization

  • Write unique title tags and meta descriptions for all static pages.
  • Research target keywords for each page so every title and description matches search intent.
  • Check that titles and descriptions follow recommended lengths and avoid duplicates across pages.
  • Validate metadata on the live site after publishing.
1.4

Indexing & Canonical Control

  • Set the global canonical URL for the website.
  • Add no-index tags to utility and low-value pages.
  • Remove non-indexable pages from the sitemap.
  • Monitor indexing status in Google Search Console.
Pedicel Marketing · 4-Phase SEO Strategy Report
Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Social Previews, Headings & Images

Workstreams 1.5 – 1.6
1.5

Open Graph & Social Sharing Settings

  • Set Open Graph titles, descriptions, and images for key pages.
  • Connect Open Graph fields to blog and case study templates.
  • Add page-specific preview images where relevant.
  • Test social sharing previews.
1.6

Heading Structure & Image SEO

  • Ensure each page has one clear H1 and logical subheadings.
  • Add descriptive alt text to all images.
  • Compress images and use modern formats such as WebP.
  • Validate headings and images across key pages.
Pedicel Marketing · 4-Phase SEO Strategy Report
Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Crawl Directives & Site Speed

Workstreams 1.7 – 1.8
1.7

XML Sitemap & Robots.txt Optimization

  • Audit and improve the XML sitemap and robots.txt directives.
  • Make sure all important, indexable pages are listed in the sitemap and can be reached by search engines.
  • Remove unsuitable or non-indexable URLs from the sitemap where needed.
  • Submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and confirm it has been processed successfully.
1.8

Website Performance & Core Web Vitals

  • Evaluate website speed and Core Web Vitals performance.
  • Find performance issues caused by images, scripts, CSS, and page load times.
  • Suggest and apply improvements wherever possible.
  • Confirm performance gains after optimization.

Phase 1 · Technical SEO

Schema & Quality Assurance

Workstreams 1.9 – 1.10
1.9

Structured Data & Schema Markup

  • Review existing structured data to find missing or incorrect schema types.
  • Add relevant schema markup that matches the website's content and business.
  • Test structured data and fix any implementation errors.
  • Confirm that schema markup correctly reflects the information on each page.
1.10

Technical Fixes & Quality Assurance

  • Implement prioritized technical fixes identified during the audit.
  • Validate crawling, indexation, redirects, canonical tags, and internal links after implementation.
  • Recheck mobile usability, HTTPS functionality, and technical errors.
  • Document completed fixes and outstanding issues.

Service Pages: Research, Content Writing & Publishing

All 4 service pages

  • Review the published homepage for content quality, layout, and indexability.

Backlinks & Digital PR

Workstreams 3.1 – 3.2

Backlink Audit & Competitor Analysis

  • Review existing backlinks and evaluate their quality and relevance.
  • Examine unusual backlink patterns and links that could cause harm.

3.2

Link Building & Digital PR

  • Find suitable link-building opportunities within the industry.
  • Reach out to relevant websites, publishers, and industry resources.
  • Secure relevant editorial links and build genuine industry relationships.
  • Track newly earned backlinks and the growth of referring domains.

Web 2.0 Link Building

Social Profiles & Google Business Profile

Workstreams 3.5 – 3.6
3.5

Social Profile Optimization

  • Identify relevant social media platforms for the business, prioritizing professional networks alongside the existing profiles.
  • Create or optimize business profiles with consistent branding, bio, logo, and messaging that reflect the agency's expertise and professional identity.
  • Add website links to key service pages, case studies, and the blog, along with relevant business details.
  • Ensure profiles accurately represent the agency and its digital marketing services.
3.6

Google Business Profile & Reviews

  • Verify business information, categories, services, and descriptions.
  • Publish regular Google Business Profile posts featuring company updates, products, services, and relevant announcements.
  • Add suitable business photos and supporting information.
  • Develop a process for requesting and responding to genuine customer reviews.
  • Monitor profile accuracy, engagement, and local search performance.
